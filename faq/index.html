Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about the shop, location, opening updates, vintage finds, and creative reuse.

Where is Vintage Expressions located?

Vintage Expressions is located at 1429 Garnet Avenue in Pacific Beach, San Diego, CA 92109.

What kind of items will be in the shop?

The shop features decorative treasures, art, furniture, clothing, reused home goods, and one-of-a-kind vintage finds.

How do I get opening updates?

Use the opening updates form on the homepage to join the early list for launch updates, first looks, and grand opening announcements.

What does creative reuse mean?

Creative reuse means giving recovered, reused, and repurposed pieces a new purpose, new setting, or new chapter.
